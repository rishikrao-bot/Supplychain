Our story

Copper, fruit and patience.

For more than half a century the distillery has turned the orchards of Transylvania into spirits: plums, apricots, quinces, pears and apples, fermented whole and distilled in copper stills. The Vampire’s ranges carry the castle’s legends into liqueurs, gin and vodka, and the newest creations pair sour cherry and blueberry liqueurs with fine chocolate.

Orchard fruitWhole fruit from the region, nothing artificial.
Copper stillsSlow, double distillation for a clean finish.
Transylvanian loreEvery range tells a story from the castle.
